Stage 3 Milestone

EKF Localization Stack Validation & Bug Fixes

Benchmarking dual Extended Kalman Filters against CARLA Ground Truth over 631 meters of driving.

The Four Critical Bugs Diagnosed in Unpatched Stack

  • ✖
    Bug 1: Unmeasured Lateral Velocity (vy):
    STM32 published vy variance as 1e6. EKF allowed unconstrained lateral wander up to 9 m/s.
    Fix: Enforced vy = 0 with tight covariance (0.001) for non-holonomic constraint.
  • ✖
    Bug 2: Local EKF Fused Fake Zero Pose:
    odom0_config fused (x, y) = (0, 0), dragging the robot back to origin.
    Fix: Set odom0 pose fusion to false; fuse twist only.
  • ✖
    Bug 3: ROS 2 Navsat IMU Remapping Silent Failure:
    Topic was remapped to imu/data (ROS 1 style). In ROS 2 Humble it is imu, causing constant datum re-initialization.
    Fix: Remapped ('imu', '/imu/data').
  • ✖
    Bug 4: Navsat Feedback Loop:
    Navsat was fed the local EKF, creating a diverging feedback loop of 944 m error.
    Fix: Fed navsat with `/odometry/global`.

Stage 4 Milestone

3D LiDAR Preprocessing & Ground Removal

Turning noisy raw point clouds into clean, segmented 3D obstacles and 2D virtual scans.

Pipeline Architecture & Algorithmic Strategy

The Perceptron 16-beam LiDAR is pitched 15° downward, meaning ground returns dominate the raw point cloud. We developed a low-latency 5-stage filter:

  • 1 Range & Ego-Self Box Filter: Strips out host car bumper reflections (dropped self points from 40% to 0.00%).
  • 2 Dual-Stage Ground Extraction: Tight RANSAC plane fitting (5 cm threshold) combined with a local-height elevation grid to preserve curbs while discarding flat road.
  • 3 2D Euclidean Clustering: Clusters non-ground points into objects. 2D projection avoids vertical beam slicing on pedestrians.
  • 4 Virtual 2D LaserScan (/perception/scan): Extracts the nearest obstacle band (0.1–1.5 m) for backward compatibility with 2D Nav2 local planners.

Research & Thesis Value

Modularity, Sim-to-Real Portability & Future Research

Why this architecture sets up Perceptron for future research papers and physical robot transfer.

1. Direct Sim-to-Real Portability

No simulator code in the autonomy graph: Every perception node subscribes strictly to standard ROS topics (PointCloud2, Image, TF).

When switching from CARLA to the physical rover on Jetson Nano, only the bridge changes from carla_bridge to jetson_robot_bridge.py. The filters, tracker, and costmap run completely unmodified.

2. Future Multi-Camera & BEV Fusion

Because Stage 6 & 8 rely on contract-based 3D boxes and voxel grids:

  • ➔ Surround View (BEV): We can replace single camera fusion with a 4-camera Bird’s-Eye-View network (e.g. BEVFormer).
  • ➔ Dual 3D LiDARs: Easily merge front & rear LiDAR streams into Stage 4.
  • ➔ Zero Downstream Changes: Tracking, Trajectory Prediction, and Nav2 remain untouched.

3. Repeatable Academic Benchmarking

Every subsystem carries its own ground-truth evaluation node:

  • ✔ localization_eval_node (6-DOF RMSE)
  • ✔ cloud_eval_node (P/R against semantic lidar)
  • ✔ tracking_eval_node (CLEAR-MOT, ID switches)
  • ✔ costmap_eval_node (Voxel recall & flicker)

Provides hard quantitative data for publications and thesis defense!
